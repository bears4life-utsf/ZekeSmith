**Status:** Living document · **Owner:** Zeke S. Smith · **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-24

This is the constitution of this repository. Every essay, diagram, interactive model, presentation, and book chapter produced here should be traceable back to something written below.

It is intentionally unfinished. Sections marked `TODO` are yours to fill. Prompts marked with `>` are questions to answer, not statements to accept.

**How to use this document**

- Read it before starting new work.
- Cite it when a decision is contested.
- Amend it when your thinking changes — do not quietly contradict it.
- Record meaningful amendments in [Revision Log](#revision-log).

## Writing Standards

How this work should feel to read.

- Plain English over consultant jargon
- Quiet confidence
- Timeless over trendy
- Evidence before opinion
- Stories that teach rather than impress
- Distinguish evidence from interpretation
- Present counterarguments honestly
- Admit uncertainty when appropriate
